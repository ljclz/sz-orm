§sz-orm-parallel — Parallel Query Executor

Based on parallel-query feature, executes multiple independent queries in parallel to reduce overall latency in complex scenarios. v4.5.0 M1 implements ParallelQueryScheduler + MergeStrategy + FailureStrategy.
